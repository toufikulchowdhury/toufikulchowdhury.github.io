Toufikul Chowdhury

I am a data science graduate researching spatial inequality in healthcare access across England. My current work links NHS maternity data at LSOA, Sub-ICB and ICB level with deprivation and travel time to examine late antenatal booking and unequal access to care.

My MSc dissertation modelled NHS England workforce trends from 2009 to 2023, focusing on midwifery and ambulance services. With a background in computer science, I build my analyses in Python.

I am looking for PhD opportunities in statistics, with particular interest in spatial statistics and statistical machine learning.
