Research

  • Spatial Health
    Quality-adjusted spatial accessibility to maternity care in England: A provider-determined measure and the limits of neighbourhood-scale inference
    Md Toufikul Islam Chowdhury and Jia Wang
    Manuscript in preparation

    A measure of spatial accessibility to maternity care that accounts for the rated quality of providers, estimated for 33,744 neighbourhoods and aggregated to 106 commissioners and 42 health systems, using road-network travel times, regulator ratings and administrative birth records.

  • Health Workforce
    Spatiotemporal workforce dynamics and forecasting in NHS England: Spotlight on midwives and ambulance staff
    Md Toufikul Islam Chowdhury
    MSc dissertation, University of Greenwich, 2024 · Supervisor: Dr Jia Wang · Mark: 80%

    An analysis of NHS England workforce statistics for midwives and ambulance staff, 2009 to 2023, by region, integrated care system and organisation, with SARIMAX forecasts to 2028. Midwifery headcount rose from 23,554 to 27,078 and support to ambulance staff from 14,159 to 28,458; in 2023 there were more managers (26,769) than ambulance staff (20,677).

Feb 2025 Took part in the CUSP London Data Dive 2025 at King's College London, a team challenge on urban waste management with 80+ students from eight universities. We linked recycling, fly-tipping, deprivation, council tax and housing data for the 33 London boroughs, and I produced the interactive borough map in Python. Recycling rates were lower in more deprived boroughs (Spearman ρ = −0.63) and in boroughs with more purpose-built flats (ρ = −0.64).
