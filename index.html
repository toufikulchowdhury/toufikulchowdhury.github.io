Toufikul Chowdhury

I am a Data Science graduate researching spatial inequalities in healthcare access across England. My current work examines maternity care using small-area health, demographic and socioeconomic data, with particular attention to geographical accessibility, deprivation and antenatal care.

My MSc research examined NHS England workforce trends from 2009 to 2023, focusing on midwifery and ambulance services. My broader research interests lie in statistical modelling, spatial analysis and statistical machine learning applied to health and population data.

I am seeking PhD opportunities in statistics and related quantitative fields, particularly in spatial statistics, spatio-temporal methods and statistical machine learning.
