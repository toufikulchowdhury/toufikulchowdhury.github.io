Data Science · Statistics · Spatial Analysis

MSc Data Science, University of Greenwich · BSc Computer Science & Engineering

My academic background spans computer science and data science, with research interests in statistical modelling, spatial analysis and machine learning. I am particularly interested in using quantitative methods to understand how healthcare provision and population inequalities vary across places and over time.

My work has developed from analysing workforce change within the NHS to investigating geographical inequalities in healthcare accessibility. This has led to a broader interest in spatial and spatio-temporal problems, and in the statistical methods needed to study complex health and population data.

Research

Maternity healthcare accessibility and inequality

Current research · England

My current research examines geographical inequalities in access to maternity care across England. I am bringing together small-area healthcare, demographic, socioeconomic and geographic data to study how accessibility differs between communities and how these patterns relate to wider population inequalities.

NHS workforce trends and forecasting

MSc research · University of Greenwich

My MSc research examined NHS England workforce change between 2009 and 2023, with particular attention to midwifery and ambulance services. I studied temporal, regional and organisational variation in the workforce and used time-series modelling to investigate longer-term trends.

Doctoral research

I am looking to pursue doctoral research in statistics, data science or a closely related quantitative field.
