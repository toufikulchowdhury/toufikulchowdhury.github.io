My academic background is in computer science and data science, with research interests spanning statistics, spatial analysis and machine learning. My research has developed from studying workforce change within the NHS to examining geographical inequalities in healthcare accessibility. This work has led to a broader interest in spatial and spatio-temporal problems, statistical learning, and the analysis of complex health and population data.

Research

Maternity healthcare accessibility and inequality

Current research · England

My current research examines geographical inequalities in access to maternity care across England. The work brings together small-area healthcare, demographic, socioeconomic and geographic data to investigate how accessibility differs between communities and how these differences relate to wider population inequalities.

NHS workforce trends and forecasting

MSc research · University of Greenwich

My MSc research examined NHS England workforce change between 2009 and 2023, with particular attention to midwifery and ambulance services. The study explored temporal, regional and organisational variation in workforce provision and used time-series modelling to investigate longer-term workforce trends.
