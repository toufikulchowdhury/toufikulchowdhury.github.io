MSc Data Science, University of Greenwich · BSc Computer Science & Engineering

My academic background is in computer science and data science, and my research interests lie at the intersection of statistics, spatial analysis and health data. I am particularly interested in understanding how healthcare provision and population inequalities vary across places and over time, and in the quantitative methods used to study these differences.

My research has developed from studying workforce change within the NHS to examining geographical inequalities in healthcare accessibility. Through this work, I have become increasingly interested in spatial and spatio-temporal problems, statistical learning, and the analysis of complex health and population data.

Research interests: spatial statistics · spatio-temporal modelling · statistical learning · healthcare accessibility · population inequalities

Research

Maternity healthcare accessibility and inequality

Current research · England

My current research examines geographical inequalities in access to maternity care across England. The work integrates small-area healthcare, demographic, socioeconomic and geographic data to investigate how accessibility differs between communities and how these patterns relate to wider population inequalities.

NHS workforce trends and forecasting

MSc research · University of Greenwich

My MSc research examined NHS England workforce change between 2009 and 2023, with a particular focus on midwifery and ambulance services. The study considered temporal, regional and organisational variation in workforce provision and used time-series modelling to investigate longer-term workforce trends.
